 HAPI 3 — teksti te fusha "Any other information"
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Kopjoje nga këtu poshtë, i gjithi:

- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -

The app requires an account. There is no guest mode, and the whole
meditation library is behind a subscription, so a newly registered account
would only show the paywall. Three demo accounts with permanent full access
have been prepared for the review, listed in the fields above.

PLEASE USE A DIFFERENT ACCOUNT ON EACH DEVICE.
The app allows only one active session per account: signing in on a second
device signs the first one out, with the message "Llogaria u hap ne nje
pajisje tjeter." ("The account was opened on another device."). This is an
intentional product rule, not a bug. That is why three separate accounts are
provided.

No other access restriction applies:
- No 2-step verification and no one-time codes.
- No email or phone confirmation is needed to sign in.
- No biometric login (no fingerprint, no face recognition).
- No QR codes or barcodes.
- No location, membership or IP restriction.
- The only permission the app requests is internet access.

How to sign in:
1. Open the app. The sign-in screen is the first screen ("Mire se u ktheve").
2. Enter the email in the first field and the password in the second.
3. Tap the purple button "Hyr" (= Sign in).
4. A short 3-step setup follows: a welcome screen, a name field (any name is
   fine) and reminder times. Tap "Vazhdo" (= Continue) through all three.
5. The full library then opens, with no payment required on these accounts.

The app interface is in Albanian. Useful words: "Hyr" = sign in,
"Regjistrohu" = register, "Fjalekalimi" = password, "Vazhdo" = continue.
